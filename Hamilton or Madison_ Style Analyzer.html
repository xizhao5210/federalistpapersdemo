Method: a Mosteller–Wallace-style function-word model. Each marker word has an approximate rate per 1,000 words in Hamilton's and Madison's Federalist essays; the page sums the log-likelihood ratio (Poisson) across markers. The rates here are rounded approximations from the classic study, not a fresh count of the full corpus, and modern prose differs from 1788 prose, so treat the result as a playful lean, not a verdict.
